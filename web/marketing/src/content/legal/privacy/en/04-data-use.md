Solely to provide the ItsBagelBot service: moderate chat, execute commands, and run engagement features. Our legal basis for processing this data is the fulfillment of our contract with you (Terms of Service) and our legitimate interest in securing and improving our service. We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we never share it with advertisers or data brokers. We disclose it only to service providers described in this policy, when needed to provide the service, when required by law, or to a successor in a business transfer as described below.

If the ItsBagelBot service, or substantially all of its operating assets, is sold, acquired, or merged with a successor operator, user accounts and configurations may transfer to that successor solely to maintain uninterrupted service on your channel. Any successor must agree in writing to honor the commitments and security standards in this policy. Broadcasters will receive at least 30 days advance notice via dashboard notifications and email before a transfer completes, during which you can delete your account at any time to prevent any transfer of your data.

Chat moderation is automated and follows the rules each broadcaster configures. Automated actions are limited to chat itself, such as removing a message or timing out a user, and have no legal or similarly significant effect on you.
