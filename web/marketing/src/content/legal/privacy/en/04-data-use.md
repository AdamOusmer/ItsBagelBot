We use your data to provide the ItsBagelBot service, including chat moderation, commands, and engagement features. Our legal basis for processing this data is the fulfillment of our contract with you (Terms of Service) and our legitimate interest in securing and improving our service. We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we never share it with advertisers or data brokers. We disclose it only to the service providers described in this policy for the purposes stated here, when required by law, or to a successor in a business transfer as described below.

We use your email address to send ItsBagelBot product news and offers only if you agree to receive them. The marketing email box is ticked when you accept the Terms of Service, and you can untick it before you continue or withdraw at any time. Your consent is the legal basis for these marketing emails.

If the ItsBagelBot service, or substantially all of its operating assets, is sold, acquired, or merged with a successor operator, user accounts and configurations may transfer to that successor solely to maintain uninterrupted service on your channel. Any successor must agree in writing to honor the commitments and security standards in this policy. Broadcasters will receive at least 30 days advance notice via dashboard notifications and email before a transfer completes, during which you can delete your account at any time to prevent any transfer of your data.

Chat moderation is automated and follows the rules each broadcaster configures. Automated actions are limited to chat itself, such as removing a message or timing out a user, and have no legal or similarly significant effect on you.
