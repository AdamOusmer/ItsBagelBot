To offer premium subscriptions, we integrate with Tebex. Tebex acts as the merchant of record for all transactions. When you initiate a checkout, we create a secure basket and redirect you to Tebex to complete the purchase. We do not process, collect, or store any payment details (like credit card numbers). We only receive the transaction ID and order status to fulfill your subscription. Tebex processes your payment data subject to their own Privacy Policy.

We use Resend, Inc. to deliver automated transactional emails, such as purchase receipts and gift notifications. When we send you such an email, Resend processes your email address and the message content for delivery purposes only, subject to Resend's own Privacy Policy.

We use Zoho Corporation's email services for non-automated correspondence, such as replies from our support team. When you email us, or we write to you directly, Zoho processes your email address and the content of the exchange to deliver and host that correspondence, subject to Zoho's own Privacy Policy. Aside from the processors described in this policy and a business transfer as described in this policy, we do not share your email address with anyone.


If a broadcaster turns on Premium voice alerts, the text to be read aloud and the chosen voice are sent to Google LLC's Gemini API, which returns the audio. We use Google's paid service, under which Google does not use that text or audio to improve its products and keeps it only for a limited period to detect abuse and meet legal obligations. We keep the text for at most 10 minutes while the audio is made and each audio clip for at most 5 minutes so the broadcaster's overlay can play it. Google processes this data under its own <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Privacy Policy</a> and the <a href="https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Gemini API Additional Terms of Service</a>.

If you enable the Govee Lights feature, you provide a Govee API key that we store encrypted and use only to control your own Govee devices. When a viewer redeems the reward, we send a power and colour command to Govee's API on your behalf, authenticated with your key. Govee processes those requests under its own Privacy Policy. Your key is never shown back to you, and you can remove it at any time from the dashboard, which deletes it from our systems.
