# Ingress: acknowledge completed effects

Date: 2026-11-16
Status: A-lite delivery contract. Owners: Sesame, ingress, messaging and affected data services.

## Delivery and migration contract

Hot pull durables have a `_v2` suffix and `bus.contract.version=2` metadata. Activation pauses unversioned AckNone durables before reading their final position, then retains them paused after the replacement exists. Deleting a legacy durable during overlap would let old pods recreate it. Retirement preserves its existing inactivity cleanup, which starts after old pods stop fetching. Equal contracts update mutable fields, including AckWait, MaxAckPending and replicas, in place. Immutable changes require another durable name; older contracts cannot mutate newer ones. Versioned and staging durables expire after one hour without pull activity. Stream discard policy is explicitly DiscardOld.

AckWait defaults to 20 seconds. A bus ticker sends InProgress only for fetched, unresolved deliveries last touched at least AckWait/3 ago, including worker queue time, asynchronous quarantine and reporter barrier time. Heartbeats publish outside the completion lock. Handler execution expires after 2x AckWait. Once execution returns, a delivery with unresolved reporter contributions or a committed effect awaiting its receipt retains InProgress only until source MaxAge. Returned, held deliveries do not contribute to oldest-delivery lag or readiness. Expired handler resolutions publish neither ACK nor NAK, since the server matches acknowledgements by stream sequence. Only successful completion acknowledges the source. A failed live completion requests NAK delays of 1, 2, 4, 8 and then 15 seconds, respecting a longer explicit dependency delay. Shutdown immediately NAKs live fetched deliveries never handed to handlers. Sesame's ten-second execution budget starts at delivery. Detached publication retains its separate three-second budget.

Handler deliveries release before their source MaxAge, reserving AckWait plus one heartbeat interval. A redelivery already inside that margin can execute until source expiry or its own 2x AckWait cutoff, whichever comes first. It must not inherit the expired attempt's liveness. Reporter generations survive source expiry: Sesame retains their completion handoffs and reply subjects, drops message payload and handler-context references, and later publishes their contributions without source redelivery. Expired sources stop renewing effect leases; owner-checked release removes leases when Valkey is available, otherwise their short TTL expires. Generation completion resolves the local effect without requiring a lease receipt after source expiry. A disconnected NATS connection stops claiming delivery liveness while held generations remain publishable. Handler expiries increment `deliveries_expired` in lane telemetry, including intervals with no successful completions, and keep readiness false for 30 seconds after the latest expiry. Fetching follows handler admission independently of reporter completion, without fixed delivery capacity caps.

Only Sesame provisions and cuts over hot streams and their durables. Projector provisions DATA and DATA_DLQ and waits for TWITCH_INGRESS on cold startup. It retries inspection until the stream exists or shutdown cancels startup. Projector has no ingress stream mutation permission. The compatible projector image and its restricted ACL must land with or before Sesame. Sesame rechecks stream configuration every 30 seconds as well as on reconnect, repairing drift left by an old reconciler. Pull subscribers refresh their source MaxAge on each heartbeat so progress expiry, lag health and new effect receipts follow the live horizon.

Sesame uses RollingUpdate with 100% surge and zero unavailable pods, allowing the entire incoming ReplicaSet to start together. Startup installs staging interest and converges topology while old pods still consume. Activation uses PauseConsumer, reads the paused consumer's final AckFloor and creates `_v2` at AckFloor+1. A newly split premium stream uses DeliverAll. The paused legacy durable remains retired and staging is deleted after the active durable exists. Concurrent staging creation and deletion are idempotent; a staging creator rechecks activation before returning so it cannot leave a recreated staging durable behind. Sesame passes its consumer group explicitly to shared provisioning. Old pods are removed only after replacement readiness; activation itself occurs during startup, without a fleet readiness barrier. The initial migration therefore requires the low-traffic release gate below.

Premium subject transfer remains a one-way compatibility boundary. Reconciliation creates the premium stream on a staging subject and a memory-backed DeliverAll staging durable, then detaches premium from TWITCH_INGRESS while preserving its previous storage budget and age limit. Old pods drain the detached premium backlog before PauseConsumer freezes its final position. Only then does reconciliation shrink the old reservation and attach premium to the new stream. Standard receives staging interest before conversion to Interest retention. Reconciliation resumes interrupted staging without deleting a stream.

NATS cannot atomically transfer a subject between streams. Publications during detach/attach can fail and receive no PubAck; this contract does not add publisher retry. Among accepted, retained messages, the residual migration loss is work already fetched by old AckNone pods at pause time: its floor has advanced even if its handler later fails or the pod dies. Unfetched old premium work drains through old pods; same-stream unfetched work starts at the paused floor in `_v2`. Rollback is a forward build only: retain the split topology, compatible explicit durables, periodic convergence and restricted projector ownership. The pre-split base image cannot boot against overlapping ingress subjects and must never be restored. Older reconcilers must not reclaim the premium subject.

Stats and analytics contributions join reporter generations directly, without per-event metrics leases, receipts or retry goroutines. Delivery completion waits for every joined generation's confirmed handoff. A crash between that handoff and the source acknowledgement, or an upstream duplicate, can count metrics once more. Chat-line timer activity and rearming run outside effect leases; their failures are non-final and never request source redelivery.

Money and mutation effects (earn, use, counters, gamble, duel, points adjustment and transfer, quotes, lighting and redemption) retain completed receipts for the longest feeding horizon plus AckWait, including the upstream retry horizon (`TwitchEffectRetention`, normally 7 minutes 20 seconds). Longer source horizons remain honored. Only the renewable owner lease has a short 12-second TTL. A committed effect retries its receipt write while its source can still be redelivered, without a fixed attempt count. Queued and delayed recipe segments join delivery completion and capture event identity before pooled handler state is released; their effect leases renew only while the source can be redelivered.

Ingress poison uses `ingress.dlq.>` on INGRESS_DLQ, a separate R3 file stream with a 32 MiB budget and one-day retention. This prefix is outside `dlq.>`, so even legacy DATA_DLQ wildcard reconcilers cannot overlap it and no DLQ deployment ordering is needed. DATA_DLQ retains its independent 256 MiB, seven-day budget. Existing letters stay in DATA_DLQ until replay or expiry. Quarantine publication runs outside handler/pump workers, reuses the subscriber's JetStream context, and requires PubAck before source acknowledgement. Every TWITCH_INGRESS consumer can publish `ingress.dlq.>`. Expiry warnings use the source stream's retention horizon.

Premium and standard reserve 768 and 1,152 MiB respectively for a 60-second age limit. The sizing envelope is 20,000 events/s combined, a 40/60 premium/standard split, and 1,400 stored bytes/event including headers and subject. That requires 641 and 961 MiB respectively before allocation headroom. All catalog memory reservations total 2,368 MiB per hub, leaving 704 MiB below the unchanged `max_mem: 3GB`, which NATS interprets as 3 GiB. Even a stricter decimal 3 GB ceiling leaves about 493 MiB. Storage byte budgets do not bound total process RSS or promise a horizon for larger events or a different split. Lane health also becomes unhealthy and emits a horizon warning when pending age reaches 75% of source MaxAge. Completing live work clears pending-age pressure; expiry retains the separate 30-second unhealthy window. Another pod's pending work cannot pin this pod's local lag through a shared AckFloor.

Autoscaling reads only the `_v2` durables. Its lag includes num_pending and num_ack_pending. At 20,000 events/s and 0.5-second completion, about 10,000 deliveries are normally awaiting acknowledgement across the fleet. Each lane targets 5,000 deliveries per replica, leaving a three-pod target of 15,000 even if all traffic uses one lane. Activation lag is 10,000; CPU remains an independent trigger. Fleet subscriber lanes drain concurrently. Sesame's 75-second termination grace covers the configured 25-second handler drain, 30-second subscriber drain and two-second acknowledgement flush, with 18 seconds left for other shutdown work.

Outgress freshness and the event-time action deadline apply only to premium and standard chat lanes. System work retains the system stream's five-minute retry horizon, whether EventAt is absent or old. Stale chat actions are logged and counted by the existing monitoring application.

## Recommendation

Use per-message AckExplicit on Sesame's premium and standard pull lanes. Acknowledge only after synchronous required effects succeed and every reporter generation the message joined has its DATA PubAck. Reporter flushes resolve completion tickets outside handler workers. Keep aggregation and existing DATA batch receipts; accept bounded replay and possible double counting across a crash. This does not promise exactly-once effects or recovery before broker acceptance.

Historical references below describe the original design baseline `f4ac5f5d5`, except the named local dependency. The delivery and migration contract above describes the current change. Firehose streams and consumers remain R3 memory. Use shared builders, propagate failures to the coarse failure path, and add neither fixed admission caps nor partial-failure cleanup loops (`AGENTS.md:35`, `AGENTS.md:37`, `AGENTS.md:38`, `AGENTS.md:39`).

## Constraints

The pre-migration pull implementation supports AckNone or AckAll and advances a cumulative floor at queue admission. The versioned contract above replaces that behavior with per-message completion. Changing only the acknowledgement policy cannot recover unpublished reporter windows.

Each of three hubs has JetStream `max_mem: 3GB` and a 5Gi pod limit (`deploy/messaging/nats-server.conf:53`, `deploy/messaging/nats.yaml:13`, `deploy/messaging/nats.yaml:111`). R3 places every memory replica on every hub. Existing memory stream budgets total 1,408 MiB, about 1.4 GiB (`pkg/bus/streams.go:34`, `pkg/bus/streams.go:77`, `pkg/bus/streams.go:96`, `pkg/bus/streams.go:108`, `pkg/bus/streams.go:120`, `pkg/bus/streams.go:131`). Reject a 120-second horizon at 20,000 events/s within this budget. MaxBytes bounds stored bytes, not RSS; delivery backlog also occupies hub memory outside pending counters (`deploy/messaging/README.md:148`).

DATA is a file-backed R3 handoff with finite retention (`pkg/bus/streams.go:56`). Earned credits and command uses commit batch receipts with SQL mutations (`app/db/loyalty/repository/earned_persist.go:62`, `app/db/commands/repository/uses.go:61`). Local dependency `fix/loyalty-counter-sql-receipts`, commit `b96a1769b`, admits counter receipts inside the mutation transaction (`app/db/loyalty/repository/counter_persist.go:41`, `app/db/loyalty/repository/counter_inbox.go:117`, at that ref). Its retention computes two hours, not 24 (`app/db/loyalty/repository/counter_retention.go:15`, at that ref). Reuse these immutable replayable batches; receipts must cover permitted DATA/DLQ replay.

Ingress sends no Nats-Msg-Id and drops ambiguous publication outcomes (`app/twitch/ingress/lib/ingress/nats/publisher/wire/single.ex:52`). A journal would require a persistent volume, a new operational unit absent today (`deploy/k8s/twitch-ingress.yaml:225`), to recover only locally received work. EventSub WebSockets cannot replay notifications missed while disconnected ([transport contract](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/eventsub/handling-websocket-events/)); reconnect currently tears down/retries (`app/twitch/ingress/lib/ingress/shard_session.ex:721`). That limited benefit does not justify this phase's added ownership. Journal, publisher IDs, effects stream and per-event SQL receipts remain out of scope.

## Phases

### 1. Expose completion

Give each message tickets for all required reporter generations, including generations reached through reporter transfers. Seal each generation's existing batch IDs and payloads; resolve tickets only after every publication receives PubAck. Later success cannot cover earlier failure. Existing retries preserve batch identity (`app/twitch/sesame/engine/counter_publication.go:45`). Remove abandonment from required reporters; an abandoned batch cannot resolve successfully (`app/twitch/sesame/engine/loyalty_reporter.go:236`).

Shorten loyalty/use windows from five seconds to 250-500 ms (`app/twitch/sesame/engine/loyalty_reporter.go:21`, `app/twitch/sesame/engine/use_reporter.go:19`). Stats currently flush channels every 30 seconds into loyalty aggregation (`app/twitch/sesame/engine/bot_stats.go:19`, `app/twitch/sesame/engine/bot_stats.go:27`, `app/twitch/sesame/engine/bot_stats.go:312`). If required, flush around one second with the transfer inside the barrier. Analytics buffers ten seconds and drops failed Valkey flushes; inclusion needs an error-returning confirmed flush around one second, not a fictional DATA PubAck (`internal/analytics/recorder.go:24`, `internal/analytics/recorder.go:219`). Otherwise explicitly exclude stats/analytics as best effort, by owner decision.

After execution retain only ack reply subjects and compact ticket state, not wires/envelopes. Long payload windows are incompatible with Sesame's 512MiB GOMEMLIMIT (`deploy/k8s/sesame.yaml:135`). Pace pulls by execution/completion progress without a fixed admission ceiling.

Propagate currently logged command/handler failures into completion (`app/twitch/sesame/engine/pipeline_handlers.go:23`, `app/twitch/sesame/engine/pipeline_handlers.go:40`). Required sequencer/asynchronous tasks must join completion after handler return (`app/twitch/sesame/engine/streamseq.go:28`, `app/twitch/sesame/modules/live.go:107`). Audit detached mutations such as timer activity (`app/twitch/sesame/engine/timers_valkey.go:588`).

Move earn/use and counter completion claims after the confirmed effect or DATA handoff (`app/twitch/sesame/modules/loyalty.go:523`, `app/twitch/sesame/engine/dispatch.go:261`, `app/twitch/sesame/engine/dispatch.go:392`). Today's `Duplicate` claims through `Seen`, so a kill can suppress unpublished deltas (`app/twitch/sesame/engine/dedup.go:57`, `pkg/idempotency/valkey_store.go:42`). Preserve upstream-duplicate suppression using Twitch event/constituent IDs and completed-effect checks; in-progress claims cannot mean completion. The confirmation-to-claim crash gap remains.

### 2. Bound replay within memory

Split premium chat from `TWITCH_INGRESS` into a memory stream consumed only by Sesame. Standard already has its own stream; premium shares with stream/status traffic (`pkg/bus/streams.go:77`, `pkg/bus/streams.go:96`). Apply Interest retention, finite MaxAge initially 45-60 seconds, and DiscardOld to both hot chat streams. Remove MaxMsgsPer from dedicated premium: DiscardNew alone does not disable per-subject eviction (`pkg/bus/streams.go:90`, `pkg/bus/stream_config.go:33`).

Provision hot staging durables before publication and give versioned durables one-hour inactivity deletion. Interest without a matching consumer does not preserve work. Size MaxBytes within unchanged max_mem alongside legacy streams during cutover, the longer outgress window, consumer state and allocation headroom. The horizon depends on this budget and real-hub validation. Alert on oldest consumer lag approaching the effective horizon: age/byte eviction is silent (`deploy/messaging/README.md:154`). Longer stalls lose events.

Raise ordinary outgress MaxAge and Duplicates together to about 60 seconds, covering AckWait plus restart. Five-second MaxAge currently clamps dedup to five seconds (`pkg/bus/streams.go:38`, `pkg/bus/stream_config.go:18`), allowing duplicate replies after crash redelivery. Retain stable event/output IDs (`app/twitch/sesame/engine/pipeline_output.go:151`). Carry original event time into outgress and reject stale actions before execution, including batch items; the current contract lacks that timestamp (`internal/domain/outgress/message.go:8`). Owners choose freshness thresholds.

Reconcile explicit push stream/status lanes through existing `bindDurable`/`laneConsumerConfig` (`pkg/bus/bus.go:55`, `pkg/bus/bus.go:127`). Four-second AckWait, six deliveries and three-second NAK cannot fit the shared stream's six-second MaxAge (`pkg/bus/concurrent_subscriber.go:65`, `pkg/bus/bus.go:27`, `pkg/bus/bus.go:275`, `pkg/bus/streams.go:86`). Align retention/retry timing so the intended retry/DLQ sequence can complete.

### 3. Activate explicit acknowledgements

Require hot pull AckExplicit, AckWait 20 seconds with bounded InProgress, MaxDeliver=-1, increasing delayed NAK for transient failure, MaxAckPending=-1 and one-hour inactivity deletion. Remove AckAll receipt floors and retire one-hop republish retry for these lanes. Ack only when phase-1 completion resolves; a kill leaves unfinished work available within retention.

Quarantine malformed/poison input through INGRESS_DLQ, requiring PubAck before source acknowledgement. Extend routing/permissions through `pkg/bus`. Unlimited transient retries must not strand poison indefinitely.

Activate after compatible consumers/dependencies exist. Update split-stream lane detection (`pkg/bus/lane_mode.go:12`). Cut over without dual-counting; rollback retains compatible explicit durables, never recreating them with DeliverNew or restoring AckNone.

## Rejected options

- **AckAll floors:** another pod's later completion can acknowledge earlier unfinished deliveries. Local floors cannot coordinate the shared durable.
- **Option C, per-event effects stream and SQL receipts:** roughly doubles broker traffic against the admission bottleneck (`deploy/messaging/README.md:148`) and projects 1.73 billion receipt rows/day at one per event at 20,000/s, before additional effects. Reuse batch receipts.
- **Interest/MaxAge=0/DiscardNew plus journal:** a stalled outgress/projector durable can fill the shared stream and reject newest chat fleet-wide, with no poison age exit (`deploy/messaging/accounts.yaml:266`, `deploy/messaging/accounts.yaml:308`). This global admission cap still permits per-subject eviction; adding a volume-backed journal does not fix it.

## Release gates and residual risks

The initial split must run at low traffic with capacity for the full surge ReplicaSet. Do not begin while either lane has unresolved effects or material backlog; keep traffic below one new pod's processing capacity until all replacement pods are ready. Horizon-lag readiness remains active during startup and can withhold readiness under sustained backlog. If that gate cannot be met, defer activation until a release provides a fleet readiness barrier. Install the compatible projector image and restricted ACL first or in the same release. These are prerequisites for activation, not a high-load cutover guarantee.

On real R3 hubs, kill Sesame at 20,000 events/s. Measure hub CPU/Raft load from explicit acks, peak hub RSS, redelivery latency, missing earned/use/command effects and duplicate replies. Inject failures before DATA PubAck, between PubAck/source ack, during reporter transfer and during DLQ publication. Verify actual consumer configuration, short windows, retained payload memory, stale-action rejection and push-lane recovery. These are gates, not capacity claims; tools/results stay outside the repository.

Residual risks: confirmed batches can regroup and double count in the PubAck-to-ack gap; stalls beyond the horizon evict events; ingress crashes lose publisher queues (`app/twitch/ingress/lib/ingress/nats/publisher.ex:181`); replies arrive about AckWait late after a kill; losing all memory replicas loses retained work. Cooldowns can suppress replayed replies (`app/twitch/sesame/engine/dispatch.go:108`, `app/twitch/sesame/engine/dispatch.go:455`); timers use processing time (`app/twitch/sesame/engine/timers_valkey.go:608`). A-lite does not reconstruct those decisions or make external actions exactly once.

## Owner decisions

- Stats and analytics are required completion effects.
- Recovery horizon, memory allocation, lag alerts, retry delay and outgress freshness thresholds, subject to release gates.
- Premium-stream and extended-DLQ ownership/permissions. Any new operational unit needs an explicit owner decision.
- Counter SQL receipt dependency and proposed 24-hour retention versus current two hours, with a replay boundary covering DATA/DLQ.
- Acceptance of residual duplicate/loss windows and compatible cutover/rollback behavior.
